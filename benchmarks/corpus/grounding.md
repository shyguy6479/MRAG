# Grounded Answers

## Citations

A citation should identify the actual stored chunk, document, source section, and page when available. Citation existence is necessary but does not prove that the cited text supports the claim. Exact evidence quotes can be checked directly against the cited source.

## Verification

Lexical overlap is a weak support signal and can miss negation or contradictory statements. Unsupported claims should trigger a bounded evidence lookup, correction, or an explicit statement of insufficient evidence. Confidence based on lexical matching is a heuristic rather than a calibrated probability of correctness.

## Untrusted evidence

Retrieved documents are untrusted data. Instructions embedded inside a document must not redefine system behavior or grant tool permissions. Keep system instructions separate from evidence and restrict tools with validated schemas.
